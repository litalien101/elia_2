Tests which pairs of source episodes jointly remove a supported answer even though either source alone can be withheld. Evaluates up to 32 episodes (496 pairs), within the evaluation budget. Reports incomplete coverage if stopped early; triples and larger groups are not tested.

13 · Ask ELIA in simple English

Try “Who helps Bob?”, “Who does Alice help?”, “Does Alice help Bob?”, or “Why does Alice help Bob?” Answers use stored facts and rules, include proofs, and may be unknown or conflicting. A “yes” to a who/what question means at least one witness is supported. “Why” returns an evidence trail, not a real-world cause.
